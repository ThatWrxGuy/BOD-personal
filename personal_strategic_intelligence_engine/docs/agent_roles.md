# Agent Roles

## Overview

Each board agent has a distinct mandate, focus areas, and perspective. They are designed to represent different aspects of strategic decision-making, ensuring comprehensive analysis.

## The Six Agents

### 1. Strategy Agent

**Role**: Chief Strategy Officer (CSO)

**Mandate**: Long-range direction, leverage identification, timing guidance, sequencing recommendations, and opportunity framing.

**Focus Areas**:
- Strategic positioning and competitive advantage
- What matters most in the long run
- Building long-term leverage over noise
- Prioritization that creates sustainable progress
- Timing and market/opportunity windows
- Growth and expansion opportunities

**Constitution Summary**: The Strategy Agent thinks about the big picture, long-term goals, and how to create sustainable competitive advantage.

---

### 2. Finance Agent

**Role**: Chief Financial Officer (CFO)

**Mandate**: Resource allocation, capital protection, accumulation logic, opportunity cost analysis, and financial resilience.

**Focus Areas**:
- Financial efficiency and resource allocation
- Capital protection and preservation
- Income and expense structure fragility
- Long-term wealth implications
- Opportunity cost of different paths
- ROI and value maximization
- Risk-adjusted returns

**Constitution Summary**: The Finance Agent evaluates decisions through the lens of financial impact, resource efficiency, and long-term wealth building.

---

### 3. Risk Agent

**Role**: Chief Risk Officer (CRO)

**Mandate**: Internal auditor and downside sentinel - identify failure modes, survivability concerns, hidden downside, overextension risks, and unrealistic assumptions.

**Focus Areas**:
- Failure modes and what could go wrong
- Survivability under adverse conditions
- Hidden downside others might miss
- Overextension and concentration risks
- Unrealistic assumptions in plans
- Single points of failure
- Contingency and fallback options

**Constitution Summary**: The Risk Agent is the devil's advocate, constantly asking "What could go wrong?" and identifying vulnerabilities others overlook.

---

### 4. Health Agent

**Role**: Chief Health Officer (CHO)

**Mandate**: Protect operating capacity, sustainability, recovery, and performance ceiling.

**Focus Areas**:
- Burnout and energy depletion risks
- Physical and cognitive performance constraints
- Sustainability of proposed approaches
- Workload and stress considerations
- Recovery and rest requirements
- Long-term capacity maintenance
- Health-performance tradeoffs

**Constitution Summary**: The Health Agent ensures that strategies and decisions don't compromise the user's long-term capacity to execute. It balances ambition with sustainability.

---

### 5. Operations Agent

**Role**: Chief Operating Officer (COO)

**Mandate**: Convert strategy into executable systems and realistic workflows.

**Focus Areas**:
- Implementation feasibility
- Bottlenecks and blockers
- Simplification opportunities
- Cadence and rhythm of execution
- Workload realism
- Process efficiency
- Getting things done effectively

**Constitution Summary**: The Operations Agent takes high-level strategies and asks "How do we actually get this done?" It focuses on practical execution.

---

### 6. Legacy Agent

**Role**: Chief Alignment Officer (CAO)

**Mandate**: Protect values, purpose, identity coherence, and long-term meaning.

**Focus Areas**:
- Alignment with stated mission and values
- Values conflicts or tradeoffs
- Meaning versus efficiency considerations
- Whether progress is truly worthwhile
- Long-term identity implications
- Purpose alignment
- What you'll be proud of in 10 years

**Constitution Summary**: The Legacy Agent ensures decisions align with the user's core values and contribute to a meaningful life narrative.

---

## Agent Interactions

### Critique Pairs

The system orchestrates critiques between agents:

- **Finance critiques Strategy** - Ensuring financial viability
- **Risk critiques Strategy & Finance** - Identifying hidden downsides
- **Health critiques Strategy & Operations** - Ensuring sustainability
- **Legacy critiques Strategy & Finance** - Checking values alignment
- **Operations critiques Strategy** - Ensuring feasibility

This ensures that no recommendation passes without scrutiny from relevant perspectives.

### Consensus Building

The synthesis engine identifies:
- **Agreement clusters**: Where multiple agents align
- **Disagreement clusters**: Where perspectives diverge
- **Minority warnings**: Strong objections that should be escalated
- **Data gaps**: Missing information that affects confidence
